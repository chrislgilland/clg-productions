Aura Tube Privacy Policy

CLG Productions LLC
Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy describes how the Aura Tube application (the "App") handles information. The App is developed by CLG Productions LLC (the "Developer").

1. The short version

Aura Tube has no server. The Developer operates no backend, no database, and no analytics service for the App. The App does not collect, store on Developer's systems, transmit to the Developer, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts with the Developer, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools in the App.

2. What stays on your device

The App stores certain information locally, in its own private storage on your device, so it can function. None of it is sent to the Developer. It is removed when you uninstall the App.

(a) Sign-in tokens. If you choose to sign in with your Google account, the authorization tokens the App receives are stored only in the App's private storage on your device. The App never sees, receives, or stores your Google password. Sign-in takes place on Google's own page in your device's browser.

(b) Preferences. Your settings (for example theme, text options, audio settings, and similar choices) are stored on your device.

(c) Watch history. The App keeps a local list of videos you have played, and your playback position in them, on your device only, so features such as Continue Watching and History can work. This history is not sent anywhere, is not the same as your YouTube history, and can be cleared from within the App at any time.

(d) Pins and similar local lists. Channels you pin, and similar organization choices, are stored on your device only.

3. What the App sends, and where

To work, the App communicates over the internet with YouTube and Google services only:

(a) Searches and video playback are requested from YouTube's public services, the same requests a web browser would make.

(b) If you sign in, the App uses the YouTube Data API to read and act on your own YouTube account at your direction: for example your subscriptions, your playlists, videos you have liked, your uploads, and actions you take in the App such as subscribing, liking, commenting, and reporting. These requests go between the App and Google, using the token described in section 2(a).

The Developer receives none of this traffic and keeps no logs of it, because the Developer runs no server for the App to send it to.

4. Information the App does not handle

The App does not access your contacts, your precise location, your photos or files outside the App, your microphone except when you use voice search (which is handled by your device's speech services), advertising identifiers for advertising purposes, or any financial information. The App contains no advertising.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and, as explained above, it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Security

Sign-in tokens and settings are held in the App's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Signing out of the App revokes the token with Google on a best-effort basis and removes the stored tokens from your device.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same address as this one, with a new effective date.
